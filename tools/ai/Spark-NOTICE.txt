Spark-X2.6-4B browser runtime

Adapted from mohdmahmodi/Spark-X2.6-4B-onnx-webgpu (Apache-2.0).
Modifications: pinned model revision, resumable persistent browser cache,
offline runtime assets, cooperative cancellation, session reuse and Pi adapter.
Model: XHToken/Spark-X2.6-4B (Apache-2.0), ONNX Q4 export by mohdmahmodi.
Bundled tokenizer: Hugging Face @huggingface/tokenizers 0.2.0 (Apache-2.0).
